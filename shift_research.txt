01 — Problem Definition
The core problem
Seasonal hiring in Quebec runs almost entirely on word of mouth and Kijiji ads. There is no
digital infrastructure built specifically for how blue collar seasonal hiring actually works. This
affects three parties simultaneously.
For employers (Company B)
• 	Post on Kijiji, receive 40 unqualified
responses or zero
• 	Best workers from last season are
unreachable
• 	No system for backup when a worker
no-shows
• 	Every season starts from zero — no
persistent records
• 	Scramble every April with no pipeline
of reliable workers
For workers
• 	Find jobs through cousins, Facebook
comments, pure luck
• 	No way to build a verifiable work
history
• 	Employment gaps between seasons
with no bridge
• 	No platform that represents their
identity and skills
• 	Invisible to employers outside their
personal network
The supplier problem — Company A
A landscaping company hires 8 workers for May through October. In November those workers
sit idle or leave for competitors. The company loses its trained workforce every winter and has
to rehire and retrain from scratch every spring. There is no mechanism to loan their idle
workforce to a complementary business during the off-season and recover them in spring.
This is the core insight that separates Shift from a job board: we are not just connecting
workers to jobs. We are enabling companies to share and circulate their workforce across
seasons without losing them.
Why existing solutions fail
Platform 	What it does 	Why it fails blue collar
Indeed 	Generic job board, resume upload 	Built for office workers. Blue collar
workers have no resume.
Jobillico 	Quebec job board, similar to Indeed 	Same problem. Treats a landscaper
like an accountant.

02 — Platform Model
The three actors
Shift is a three-sided marketplace. Every transaction involves all three parties simultaneously.
Company A — Supplier 	Shift — Platform 	Company B — Buyer
Has employees on payroll
Has slow seasons where
workers sit idle
Wants to retain workers year-
round
Wants workers back in spring
Connects supply and demand
Handles all payments via
Stripe
Takes percentage commission
Manages trust and reliability
Needs workers urgently
Does not want to hire full-time
Wants verified reliable workers
Pays Shift for placements
How the worker flows
The worker is employed by Company A. They are delegated to Shift during the off-season. Shift
places them at Company B. When Company A's season returns, the worker goes back. The
worker never experiences an employment gap.
01 Company A adds workers to Shift pool
Specifies which workers are available, for which sectors, and for what date range.
02 Company B posts a shift need
Specifies skills needed, dates, location, hours, and pay rate. Takes 3 minutes.
03
Shift matches and surfaces top worker
Algorithm scores available workers by skill, location, reliability, and availability. Shows best
match to Company B.
04
Company A manually approves the placement
Receives a notification. Reviews the shift details. Approves or declines. Manual at MVP —
automated later.
05 Worker receives shift notification via SMS
Company B name, location, hours, pay. One tap to accept or decline.

-- 4 of 15 --

06 Worker clocks in via GPS
Opens app at Company B location. GPS verifies presence. Shift timer starts.
07
Worker clocks out — payment triggers automatically
Hours calculated. Stripe Connect splits payment: 15% to Shift, rest to Company A. Company B
charged.
08
Both parties rate the experience
Worker rates employer. Employer rates worker. Reliability scores update. System ready for
next placement.
Payment flow
All payments flow through Stripe Connect. Shift never touches money manually. Every split,
payout, and receipt is automated.
Company B pays 	Shift keeps 	Company A receives
$200.00 (8hrs @ $25/hr) 	$30.00 (15% commission) 	$170.00 (pays worker from
this)
Stripe Connect handles: split payments, automatic payouts, KYC verification, tax
documents, fraud detection, and Canadian banking compliance. Shift never stores banking
credentials.

03 — Market Research
Why Quebec
Quebec has a specific combination of conditions that makes it the ideal first market for Shift.
• 	Severe skilled trades shortage — construction industry needs tens of thousands of
workers over the next decade
• 	DEP graduates receiving job offers before completing their program
• 	Highly seasonal economy — landscaping, agriculture, ski resorts, construction, festivals
all have distinct seasons that complement each other
• 	Hydro-Quebec making Quebec a top data center destination — driving even more
demand for trades workers
• 	French-first market that English platforms serve poorly — local advantage for a bilingual
platform
• 	No digital infrastructure exists for blue collar seasonal work specifically

05 — Technical Architecture
Stack decision
One repo. One deployment. Clean internal folder separation by actor. This is a monolith with
microservice-ready structure — not actual microservices. The overhead of 3 repos would slow
MVP by months with zero benefit at this scale.
Layer 	Tool 	Why
Frontend 	Next.js + TypeScript +
Tailwind
One codebase for web and mobile web. App router.
Already in your stack.
Backend 	Next.js API routes 	No separate Express server needed. Routes map to
/worker, /employer, /system, /shared folders.
Database 	PostgreSQL + Prisma 	Type-safe queries, clean migrations, perfect with
TypeScript. Railway for hosting.
Payments 	Stripe Connect 	The only tool built for this exact model. Handles splits,
payouts, KYC, tax docs, Canadian banking.
SMS 	Twilio 	Blue collar workers read texts not emails. Every
notification is an SMS. $0.0079 per message.
GPS clock-in 	Browser Geolocation
API
Free. No extra tool. Worker opens app at site, GPS
verifies location match.
Auth 	NextAuth.js 	Phone + SMS OTP via Twilio. No email or password
needed for workers.
Admin panel 	Retool 	Drag and drop internal dashboard. Connects to your
PostgreSQL directly. No code needed.
Analytics 	PostHog 	Free tier. User behaviour, funnel drop-off, feature
usage. Required before any investor pitch.
Deployment 	Vercel + Railway 	Frontend on Vercel. Database on Railway. Under
$15/month until serious scale.

Get Company B to post a shift before opening worker sign-ups. Get Company A to add
workers before Company B can see them. The platform must never be empty when a new
actor signs up for the first time.

07 — Key Rules
These are the principles that should never be violated regardless of what happens during build
or launch.
01
Never touch CCQ regulated trades at MVP
Electricians, HVAC, plumbers — CCQ regulated. Legal exposure is startup-ending.
Manoeuvres construction is your safe entry into trades.
02
Solve chicken and egg with B2B first
Get 10 Company B shift postings live before opening to Company A workers. Nobody joins an
empty platform.
03
Manual approval before automation
Learn how Company A approves manually for 2 months. That behavior becomes the logic for
auto-approve later.
04
Stripe Connect is non-negotiable
Never build your own payment split logic. Stripe Connect was built exactly for this model. Use it
from day one.
05
SMS over push notifications
Blue collar workers read texts in 3 minutes. They do not check app notifications. Every critical
trigger is an SMS via Twilio.
06
Reliability score is the moat
It is what makes Shift defensible against Kijiji and Indeed. Build it right from day one even if it is
just a simple average. The data compounds.
07
One city before one province
Montreal landscaping only at launch. Dominate one micro-market completely before expanding.
Spreading thin kills marketplaces.
08
Traction before funding
Investors want to see you talked to real people and something is live. Do not pitch before 4
weeks of real usage data and real numbers.
